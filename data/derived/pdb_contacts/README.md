# Heteromeric PDB Interface Contacts Of DisProt Proteins

- Generated at (UTC): `2026-11-23T16:01:53Z`
- Generated by: `scripts/19_pdb_contacts.jl`

One row per (DisProt protein, PDB entry). Protein chains come from
`disprot_pdb_mapping.csv.gz`; partner chains are the other polymer chains of
the first model that are not copies of the protein (SIFTS chains, or k-mer
identity to the protein sequence). A protein residue is in contact when one of
its heavy atoms (hydrogens removed) is within the contact cutoff of a heavy
atom of a partner chain. Positions are UniProt positions (SIFTS XML mapping),
written as ranges such as `12-40;55`. All contacts are reported, without
disorder or PDB quality filter.

## Columns

- `protein_disprot_id`: DisProt identifier of the dataset protein (key shared with the other derived CSVs)
- `protein_id`: UniProt accession of the dataset protein
- `PDB`: PDB entry code (lowercase)
- `method`: experimental method from the PDB quality table (`missing` if absent)
- `resolution`: resolution in Å from the PDB quality table (empty if absent or not applicable)
- `quality_category`: `resolved` (resolution known), `no_resolution` (e.g. NMR) or `absent` (not in the quality table)
- `status`: `ok`, `no_heteromeric_partner`, `no_mmcif`, `no_sifts`, `no_target_chain` or `no_mapped_residue` (see `PDB_CONTACT_STATUSES`)
- `chains`: protein chains of the PDB entry used (SIFTS chains present in the mmCIF, `;`-separated)
- `partner_chains`: heteromeric partner chains (polymer chains that are not copies of the protein)
- `homo_chains_excluded`: other copies of the protein excluded from partners (k-mer identity)
- `identity`: fraction of observed protein residues mapped to UniProt (all protein chains) whose amino acid matches the UniProt sequence (`NaN` if none); entries below `PDB_CONTACT_MIN_MAPPING_IDENTITY` are not used for the aggregated labels
- `mismatch_positions`: UniProt positions (ranges) where an observed residue of a protein chain differs from the UniProt sequence (mutations, modified residues); not used for the aggregated labels, and an entry with one on the DisProt binding region ± `BINDING_REGION_FLANK` residues is not used
- `resolved_positions`: UniProt positions observed in the structure, as ranges (`12-40;55`)
- `n_contacts`: number of distinct UniProt positions in contact with a partner chain
- `contact_positions`: UniProt positions in contact with a partner chain (heavy atoms within the cutoff), as ranges; all residues, not only disordered ones
