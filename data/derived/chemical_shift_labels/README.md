# Residue Labels From BMRB Chemical Shifts

- Generated at (UTC): `2026-11-23T15:30:40Z`
- Generated by: `scripts/22_chemical_shift_labels.jl`

Disorder is called on free-state shift lists from secondary shifts
Δδ(Cα) − Δδ(Cβ) (random coil: ncidp) and the amide
1H window; binding on pairs of a bound list with its most similar comparable
free list, from chemical-shift perturbations (CSP) and lost peaks in runs of
consecutive residues. `chemical_shift_lists.csv.gz` gives, for every shift list,
whether it was used and why; `chemical_shift_comparisons.csv.gz` gives every
bound × free comparison with each criterion (`same`, `different`, `unknown`),
`accepted` (no required criterion differs) and `selected` (pair used), and
the buffering species of both samples (buffers are compared on their species
only: additives and salts are ignored, concentrations may differ). Shift lists
need an entity identical to UniProt on the DisProt binding region ±
3 residues and at least
0.95 identical overall; mutated residues elsewhere
are not used.

## Columns

- `protein_disprot_id`: DisProt identifier of the dataset protein (key shared with the other derived CSVs)
- `protein_id`: UniProt accession of the dataset protein
- `n_free_lists`: free-state shift lists used for disorder
- `n_pairs`: free/bound pairs used for binding
- `free_lists`: free lists used (`bmrb_id:shift_list_id`, `;`-separated)
- `pairs`: free/bound pairs used (`free>bound`)
- `disprot`: DisProt residue labels (`0`/`1`/`2`), for comparison
- `cs_n_free`: free lists where the residue disorder could be evaluated
- `cs_n_pairs`: pairs where the residue is observed (CSP or lost peak)
- `Label_chemical_shift`: `0` ordered, `1 + p_binding` disordered (`p_disorder ≥ p_disorder_threshold`; `1` when no pair covers the residue); `NaN` without NMR data
- `Label_chemical_shift_disorder`: `p_disorder`: fraction of free lists calling the residue disordered (secondary shifts and amide 1H window)
- `Label_chemical_shift_binding`: `p_binding`: fraction of pairs calling the residue binding (CSP or lost peak runs), on NMR-disordered residues covered by a pair; `NaN` otherwise
