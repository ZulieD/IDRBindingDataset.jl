# Final Residue Binding Labels (DisProt, UniProt, PDB, Chemical Shift, SLiM)

- Generated at (UTC): `2026-11-23T16:04:32Z`
- Generated by: `scripts/24_final_labels.jl`

One row per dataset protein. Each vector column has one value per residue of
the UniProt sequence. Disorder `d` is the mean disorder vote of DisProt (`0`/`1`)
and NMR (`Label_chemical_shift_disorder`, when evaluable). On residues with
`d > 0`, binding `b = x / n` where `n` is the number of sources covering the
residue and `x` the sum of their binding votes: DisProt (`1` if labelled binding), UniProt (`1` inside a binding
site, when the protein has UniProt binding sites), PDB (`Label_PDB_binding`,
fraction of used PDB entries in contact with a heteromeric partner), chemical
shift (`Label_chemical_shift_binding`, fraction of free/bound pairs calling the
residue binding) and SLiM (`Label_SLiM`, `1` on ELM motif residues; no vote
elsewhere). Each covering source weighs the same (`1/5` when all five cover the
residue). `Label_aggregate_all = d + b` in `[0, 2]`; residues with
`d = 0` get `0`. `final_labels_sources.csv.gz` lists, per protein, the sources
available, DisProt/NMR disorder disagreements and how many disordered residues
are covered by 1 to 5 binding sources.

## Columns

- `protein_disprot_id`: DisProt identifier of the dataset protein (key shared with the other derived CSVs)
- `protein_id`: UniProt accession of the dataset protein
- `Label_aggregate_all`: `Label_aggregate_all_disorder + Label_aggregate_all_binding` in `[0, 2]`: `0` ordered, `1` disordered, `2` disordered binding (all sources agree); `0` where no source calls the residue disordered
- `Label_aggregate_all_disorder`: `d` in `[0, 1]`: mean disorder vote of DisProt (`0`/`1`) and NMR (`Label_chemical_shift_disorder`, when evaluable); e.g. `0.5` when DisProt calls the residue disordered and NMR ordered
- `Label_aggregate_all_binding`: `b` in `[0, 1]` on residues with `d > 0`: mean binding vote of the covering sources (DisProt `0`/`1`, UniProt `0`/`1`, `Label_PDB_binding`, `Label_chemical_shift_binding`, `Label_SLiM` `1` on motif residues only); `NaN` where `d = 0`
- `(final_labels_sources.csv.gz) protein_disprot_id`: DisProt identifier of the dataset protein
- `(final_labels_sources.csv.gz) protein_id`: UniProt accession of the dataset protein
- `(final_labels_sources.csv.gz) sources`: sources with at least one covered residue (`disprot`, `uniprot`, `pdb`, `chemical_shift`, `slim`; `;`-separated)
- `(final_labels_sources.csv.gz) n_disordered`: residues called disordered by at least one source (`d > 0`)
- `(final_labels_sources.csv.gz) n_partial_disorder`: residues where DisProt and NMR disagree on disorder (`0 < d < 1`)
- `(final_labels_sources.csv.gz) n_nmr_only_disorder`: residues ordered in DisProt but disordered for NMR
- `(final_labels_sources.csv.gz) n_1_source`: disordered residues with DisProt as only binding source
- `(final_labels_sources.csv.gz) n_2_sources`: disordered residues covered by 2 binding sources
- `(final_labels_sources.csv.gz) n_3_sources`: disordered residues covered by 3 binding sources
- `(final_labels_sources.csv.gz) n_4_sources`: disordered residues covered by 4 binding sources
- `(final_labels_sources.csv.gz) n_5_sources`: disordered residues covered by all 5 binding sources
